İki baba kalp kırıklığının arasına bir din psikozu sığdırdım. I felt the presence of God while I was making bad choices, as if He were encouraging me, but I couldn't hear His voice afterward. I went through a religious psychosis between two major heartbreaks. Ortadan ikiye, üçe ayrılacağımı hissettim. Por fin, se acabó. There is no direct way to learn about God now, with every SparkNote that dissects yet another soul fragment dipped in paranoia-inducing honey. There are preachers, though. Not so hidden under indirect lighting, blurring out any sharp features so I don't cut myself or any other soldier. I present myself shyly, only one foot touching the ground. I listen and I refrain because I know exactly what you're talking about; I wouldn't want to shatter the image. The facade. You know it's fake. You could smell it on me. Şeytanın tavsiyesini dinledim. İnanıyor gibi görünürsen daha çok insanı yoldan çıkarabiliyorsun. I said there is no point in being this alluring if all it does is act as a disadvantage. You disagree. You believed in Him when we first met. I watched you drift away. "Biliyor musun," demiştim, "belki bir gün ben de inanırım." Annem zaten yaşlandıkça içimdeki siyah noktanın büyüyeceğini ve bir gün o boşluğu Allah hariç hiçbir şeyle dolduramayacağını söylemişti. I watched as your black spot became a shroud, and then a void. When I saw that the void in your consciousness became so big even God had trouble filling it up, and when you had second thoughts about your belief, I left you. To find a new disciple...
